# campusconnect-team

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

CampusConnect is a fictional campus event finder designed to help students easily discover and stay informed about activities happening within their school. It features different types of campus events, including seminars, workshops, sports competitions, student organization activities, and school celebrations.

The project aims to provide students with an accessible way to explore upcoming events, view important event details, and stay updated on school activities. Through CampusConnect, students can become more involved in campus life and avoid missing events that match their interests.

FEATURES

1. CampusConnect displays a list of upcoming campus events so students can easily see the available activities in their school.
2. Students can search for specific events by entering an event name or keyword.
3. Students can browse events based on categories such as academic activities, sports, social events, and student organization activities.
4. Students can view important information about each event, including its title, date, time, location, and description.
5. CampusConnect helps students identify upcoming activities so they can plan their schedules and avoid missing important events.
6. CampusConnect presents event information in a simple and organized layout so students can easily find the information they need.

HOW TO USE

1. Visit the CampusConnect Website. Open the CampusConnect website using your preferred web browser.
2. Register or Log In. Create an account if you are a new user. If you already have an account, enter your login credentials to access the platform.
3. Explore Available Events. Browse the list of upcoming campus events and activities posted on the website.
4. Search for Specific Events. Use the search bar to enter an event name or keyword to quickly find a particular activity.
5. Browse Events by Category. Explore different event categories, such as academic, sports, and social activities, to find events that match your interests.
6. View Event Details. Select an event to see important information, including its name, date, time, location, and description.
7. Register for an Event. Choose your preferred event and click the registration button. Fill in the required information and submit your registration.
8. Confirm Your Registration. Review your registration details and check for a confirmation message to ensure that your registration was successful.
9. Attend the Event. Take note of the event's date, time, and location so you can arrive on time and participate in the activity.
